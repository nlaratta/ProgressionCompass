Progression Compass — always know what to do next.

Stop guessing what to craft, where to farm, and which boss to fight. Progression Compass scans your inventory and tells you exactly what upgrades are within reach, what materials you're missing, and where to get them.

What makes it different:
- Knows what you OWN — scans inventory, banks, equipment, chests, and Magic Storage
- Shows what's NEXT — gear upgrades ranked by your class, tier, and current loadout
- Tells you WHERE — action plans grouped by location so you make one trip, not ten
- Tracks your GOALS — pin any item and get a persistent HUD overlay with progress

Key features:
- "Ready to Craft" / "Almost Ready" / "Building Toward" dashboard
- Class-aware upgrade recommendations (Melee/Ranged/Mage/Summoner)
- Boss progression with summon hints (integrates with Boss Checklist)
- Step-by-step craft order with source info, drop rates, and alternates
- TODO action plan: what to farm, buy, craft, and which events to trigger
- Shimmer transforms, shop prices/conditions, expert/master drop tagging

Works automatically with all loaded mods (Calamity, Thorium, etc.).
Pairs well with Recipe Browser — we handle progression planning, it handles recipe browsing.

Client-side only — no server impact in multiplayer.
