Craft Path Optimizer computes the optimal crafting path for any item in the game.

Select a target item and instantly see:
- Full dependency tree from raw materials to final product
- Material checklist showing what you own vs. what you need
- Where to get missing materials (drops, shops, fishing, etc.)
- Step-by-step crafting order

Works automatically with all loaded mods (Calamity, Thorium, etc.).
Scans your full inventory, banks, equipment, and optionally nearby chests.
Queue multiple targets to see a unified material list.

Client-side only — no server impact in multiplayer.
